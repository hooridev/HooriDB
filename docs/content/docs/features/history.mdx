Every statement you run from a console is saved in that connection's history (up to 1,000 statements per connection; statements larger than 64 KB are not recorded). History is stored in `history.json`, readable only by your user account on Linux.

## Open History

While a console tab is active, open its connection's history in either of two ways:

- Press the **History** (clock) icon in the console toolbar.
- Choose **File > History…**.

Statements appear newest first with four columns: `Statement` (the first non-blank line), `Ran`, `Took`, and `Outcome` (`Rows`, `N rows changed`, `Cancelled`, or the error message).

## Search and insert a statement

1. Type in the **Search** box to filter statements containing that text (case-insensitive).
2. Select a row and press **Insert**, double-click the row, or press `Enter`. You can also copy selected rows with `Ctrl+C`.

If you have text selected in the console editor, the inserted statement replaces the selection. Otherwise, HooriDB inserts it as a separate statement on its own line (after the statement the caret is inside, adding a semicolon to the previous statement if needed) and places the caret right after it so you can run it right away. You can undo the insert in one step.

## Clear history

1. Press **Clear history** in the History dialog.
2. Press **Clear** to confirm.

Clearing deletes all saved statements for that connection from disk. Open consoles keep their text. Deleting a connection from Workspace also removes its history.
